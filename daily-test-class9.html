Computational Thinking

Posted in 9 Computer Science

These lecture notes are prepared by Salman Ahmad, who is both a teacher and a web developer. The purpose of this material is to make the topic clear and useful for students of Class 9 Computer Science.

Practice MCQs

1. What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?
a. A computer virus
b. The ability of machines to perform intelligent tasks
c. A type of monitor
d. A storage device
Answer: b. The ability of machines to perform intelligent tasks
2. What is an algorithm?
a. A hardware component
b. A random process
c. A step-by-step procedure to solve a problem
d. A computer game
Answer: c. A step-by-step procedure to solve a problem
3. Which field focuses on making machines intelligent?
a. Networking
b. Artificial Intelligence
c. Web Designing
d. Animation
Answer: b. Artificial Intelligence
4. What is the purpose of algorithms?
a. To damage computers
b. To solve problems efficiently
c. To increase hardware size
d. To create viruses
Answer: b. To solve problems efficiently
5. Which of the following is a technique used in AI?
a. Machine Learning
b. Painting
c. Typing
d. Printing
Answer: a. Machine Learning
6. What does Machine Learning allow computers to do?
a. Learn from data
b. Repair hardware
c. Increase screen size
d. Delete files automatically
Answer: a. Learn from data
7. Which technique helps AI systems make decisions?
a. Cooking
b. Reasoning
c. Drawing
d. Typing
Answer: b. Reasoning
8. What is a Black Box AI system?
a. An AI system whose internal working is hidden
b. A black-colored computer
c. A damaged AI system
d. A hardware box
Answer: a. An AI system whose internal working is hidden
9. What is a White Box AI system?
a. A white-colored robot
b. An AI system whose decisions can be understood
c. A storage device
d. A computer case
Answer: b. An AI system whose decisions can be understood
10. Which AI type provides clear explanations of its decisions?
a. Black Box AI
b. White Box AI
c. Hidden AI
d. Automatic AI
Answer: b. White Box AI
11. Which AI type is difficult to understand internally?
a. White Box AI
b. Open AI
c. Black Box AI
d. Simple AI
Answer: c. Black Box AI
12. Which of the following is an important AI technique?
a. Problem Solving
b. Washing
c. Driving
d. Painting
Answer: a. Problem Solving
13. Why are algorithms important in AI?
a. They help computers solve tasks logically
b. They increase monitor size
c. They clean hardware
d. They reduce electricity
Answer: a. They help computers solve tasks logically
14. Which AI system is more transparent?
a. Black Box AI
b. White Box AI
c. Hidden AI
d. Automatic AI
Answer: b. White Box AI
15. What is the main purpose of AI?
a. To make machines intelligent
b. To reduce computer speed
c. To damage systems
d. To increase hardware weight
Answer: a. To make machines intelligent
