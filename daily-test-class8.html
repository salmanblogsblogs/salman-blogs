Data Communication and Computer Network

Posted in 8 Computer Science

These lecture notes are prepared by Salman Ahmad, who is both a teacher and a web developer. The purpose of this material is to make the topic clear and useful for students of Class 9 Computer Science.

Practice MCQs

1. What does VPN stand for?
a. Virtual Private Network
b. Visual Public Network
c. Virtual Protected Node
d. Verified Personal Network
Answer: a. Virtual Private Network
2. What is the main purpose of a VPN?
a. To secure internet connections
b. To repair computers
c. To increase battery life
d. To clean devices
Answer: a. To secure internet connections
3. Which of the following is a benefit of using a VPN?
a. Protecting online privacy
b. Deleting files automatically
c. Charging devices faster
d. Increasing screen size
Answer: a. Protecting online privacy
4. VPNs are commonly used when accessing:
a. Public Wi-Fi networks
b. Printed books
c. Offline games
d. USB devices
Answer: a. Public Wi-Fi networks
5. What is data analytics?
a. Designing websites
b. Repairing hardware
c. Examining data to find useful information
d. Installing software
Answer: c. Examining data to find useful information
6. Why is data analytics important?
a. It helps make informed decisions
b. It damages storage devices
c. It reduces internet signals
d. It changes hardware automatically
Answer: a. It helps make informed decisions
7. Which tool is commonly used in data analytics?
a. Spreadsheet software
b. Television remote
c. Printer cable
d. Microwave oven
Answer: a. Spreadsheet software
8. VPN helps users by:
a. Hiding their online activity
b. Breaking computers
c. Removing passwords
d. Deleting internet history permanently
Answer: a. Hiding their online activity
9. Data analytics is mainly used to:
a. Play online games
b. Find patterns and insights in data
c. Charge devices faster
d. Create computer viruses
Answer: b. Find patterns and insights in data
10. Which activity is safer while using public Wi-Fi?
a. Browsing news websites
b. Online banking
c. Entering passwords
d. Shopping online
Answer: a. Browsing news websites
11. Which number from the data set 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13 is not divisible by 2?
a. 4
b. 8
c. 11
d. 10
Answer: c. 11
12. In the data set 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, which numbers are divisible by 5?
a. 10 and 20 only
b. All numbers
c. 5 and 15 only
d. 25 only
Answer: b. All numbers
13. Which number is the highest in the data set 12, 18, 25, 7, 30?
a. 18
b. 25
c. 30
d. 12
Answer: c. 30
14. Which number is the smallest in the data set 14, 9, 22, 31, 18?
a. 14
b. 9
c. 18
d. 22
Answer: b. 9
15. Which numbers in the data set 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 are divisible by 3?
a. 3 and 6 only
b. 9 and 12 only
c. 15 only
d. All numbers
Answer: d. All numbers
16. What is the average of the numbers 10, 20, 30, 40?
a. 20
b. 25
c. 30
d. 35
Answer: b. 25
17. Which number appears most often in the data set 2, 4, 4, 6, 8, 4?
a. 2
b. 6
c. 4
d. 8
Answer: c. 4
18. In the data set 11, 22, 33, 44, 55, which numbers are divisible by 11?
a. 22 and 44 only
b. 11 and 33 only
c. 55 only
d. All numbers
Answer: d. All numbers
19. Which number is missing in the pattern 5, 10, 15, __, 25?
a. 18
b. 20
c. 22
d. 30
Answer: b. 20
20. Which number from the data set 7, 14, 21, 28 is divisible by 7?
a. 14 only
b. 21 only
c. 28 only
d. All numbers
Answer: d. All numbers
