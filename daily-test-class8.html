Data Communication and Computer Network

Posted in 8 Computer Science

These lecture notes are prepared by Salman Ahmad, who is both a teacher and a web developer. The purpose of this material is to make the topic clear and useful for students of Class 9 Computer Science.

Practice MCQs

1. What is data communication?
a. Storing data in a computer
b. Exchanging data between devices
c. Writing programs
d. Printing documents
Answer: b. Exchanging data between devices
2. Who sends the data in communication?
a. Receiver
b. Sender
c. Decoder
d. Medium
Answer: b. Sender
3. Who receives the data?
a. Sender
b. Encoder
c. Receiver
d. Signal
Answer: c. Receiver
4. What is a message in data communication?
a. Device
b. Data or information being sent
c. Cable
d. Software
Answer: b. Data or information being sent
5. What is transmission media?
a. A type of computer
b. Path through which data travels
c. A programming tool
d. A storage device
Answer: b. Path through which data travels
6. Which of the following is a transmission medium?
a. Keyboard
b. Monitor
c. Optical fiber
d. Printer
Answer: c. Optical fiber
7. What does an encoder do?
a. Receives data
b. Converts data into signals
c. Deletes data
d. Stores data
Answer: b. Converts data into signals
8. What does a decoder do?
a. Sends data
b. Converts signals back into data
c. Connects devices
d. Stores signals
Answer: b. Converts signals back into data
9. Which component is responsible for understanding the message?
a. Sender
b. Receiver
c. Encoder
d. Medium
Answer: b. Receiver
10. What is required for communication to occur?
a. Only sender
b. Only receiver
c. Sender, receiver, and medium
d. Only data
Answer: c. Sender, receiver, and medium
11. Which of the following is NOT a component of data communication?
a. Sender
b. Receiver
c. Compiler
d. Medium
Answer: c. Compiler
12. What form does data take during transmission?
a. Signals
b. Files
c. Images
d. Documents
Answer: a. Signals
13. Which device helps in converting digital data into signals?
a. Decoder
b. Encoder
c. Printer
d. Scanner
Answer: b. Encoder
14. Which medium uses air to transmit data?
a. Wired
b. Wireless
c. Optical
d. Cable
Answer: b. Wireless
15. What is the main purpose of data communication?
a. To store data
b. To share information
c. To delete files
d. To design software
Answer: b. To share information
